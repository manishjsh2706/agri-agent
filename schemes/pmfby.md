# PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)

**Also known as:** PMFBY, PM Fasal Bima Yojana, प्रधानमंत्री फसल बीमा योजना, प्रधानमंत्री पीक विमा योजना, Crop Insurance Scheme.

**Level:** Central Government of India scheme.
**Ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.
**Launched:** 13 January 2016 (replaced older schemes NAIS and MNAIS).
**Official website:** https://pmfby.gov.in
**Farmer helpline / grievance portal:** https://pmfby.gov.in (Farmer Corner)
**Last verified:** July 2026.

---

## What is PMFBY

PMFBY is a Central Government crop insurance scheme that protects farmers against financial loss due to crop damage. If a farmer's crop yield falls below a threshold due to natural calamities, pests, or diseases, the scheme pays a claim amount to compensate.

The scheme covers ALL food crops (cereals, pulses, oilseeds), commercial crops, and horticulture crops for which past yield data is available. It is implemented in every state and union territory of India through empanelled insurance companies selected by state governments.

---

## Farmer Premium (What the Farmer Pays)

Farmers pay only a small share of the total insurance premium; the rest is subsidised by the Central and State Governments.

- **Kharif (monsoon) crops:** Farmer pays **2%** of sum insured
- **Rabi (winter) crops:** Farmer pays **1.5%** of sum insured
- **Commercial and horticulture crops:** Farmer pays **5%** of sum insured

Example: If a farmer's sum insured for kharif paddy is Rs 50,000, the farmer pays 2% = Rs 1,000 as premium. The rest of the actuarial premium (which can be much higher) is paid by the government.

---

## What is Covered

PMFBY covers **yield losses** caused by:

- **Natural fire and lightning**
- **Storm, hailstorm, cyclone, typhoon, tempest, hurricane, tornado**
- **Flood, inundation, and landslide**
- **Drought and dry spells**
- **Pests and diseases** (widespread outbreak)

Two localised risks are also covered under specific add-on modules:
- **Prevented sowing** (if adverse weather prevents sowing during the notified area's window)
- **Post-harvest losses** (up to 14 days after harvest, for crops kept in "cut and spread" condition to dry)

---

## Who is Eligible

The following farmers are eligible for PMFBY:

- All farmers growing notified crops in notified areas — **both loanee and non-loanee** farmers
- **Sharecroppers and tenant farmers** growing notified crops (with valid land documents)
- Farmers must apply during the enrolment window (usually 2-3 months before the season starts)

**Important — voluntary since 2020:** From Kharif 2020 onwards, PMFBY is **voluntary** for all farmers, including those who took crop loans from banks. Loanee farmers must specifically opt IN (or can opt OUT) at the bank.

---

## Who is NOT Covered

PMFBY does **not** cover:

- Losses due to **war, nuclear risks, or malicious damage**
- **Voluntary destruction** of crops
- Losses in crops that are NOT notified for that particular area or season
- Farmers who missed the enrolment window
- Losses from causes other than the perils listed above (e.g., theft, animal grazing)

---

## How to Apply / Enrol

There are four ways to enrol in PMFBY:

**1. Online through the National Crop Insurance Portal**
- Go to https://pmfby.gov.in → "Farmer Corner" → "Apply for Crop Insurance"
- Enter Aadhaar, land details, crop, and season
- Pay premium online (UPI, net banking, card)

**2. Through your bank (for loanee farmers)**
- Farmers with Kisan Credit Card / crop loans can enrol at their bank
- Premium is auto-debited from the loan account (if farmer opts IN)

**3. Common Service Center (CSC)**
- Visit nearest CSC and provide Aadhaar and land documents
- VLE (Village Level Entrepreneur) fills the online form

**4. Insurance company agent or state agriculture officer**
- Notified insurance company agents visit villages during enrolment window
- State agriculture officers can also enrol farmers

**Enrolment deadline:** Every state notifies its own cut-off date for each crop and season. Farmers must apply BEFORE this deadline. Late applications are not accepted.

---

## Required Documents

Keep these documents ready:

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory)
- **Bank passbook** with account number and IFSC
- **Land ownership records** (7/12 extract, patta, or khasra)
- For **tenant / sharecropper farmers:** valid tenancy or sowing certificate from village revenue officer
- **Sowing certificate** or self-declaration of crop sown (in some states)
- **Mobile number** (for OTP and claim SMS updates)

## How Claims Work

Claims under PMFBY are triggered in two ways:

**1. Widespread yield loss (area-based)**
- If Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) show the actual yield in the notified area is lower than the "threshold yield" — a claim is paid to ALL insured farmers of that crop in that area
- Claim amount = (Threshold Yield - Actual Yield) / Threshold Yield × Sum Insured
- Typically settled 2-3 months after harvest

**2. Localised loss (individual farm)**
- For losses due to hailstorm, landslide, cyclone, or inundation, individual farms can claim
- Farmer must **report the loss within 72 hours** of the event (via toll-free helpline, insurance company, bank, or CSC)
- Insurance company appoints a loss surveyor to assess damage
- Claim paid based on survey report

Claim amount is directly credited to the farmer's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

---

## How to Report a Crop Loss (72-Hour Rule)

If your crop is damaged by hailstorm, cyclone, landslide, or inundation:

1. Report loss within **72 hours** of the event
2. Report via any of these channels:
   - Insurance company's dedicated helpline (number provided at enrolment)
   - Bank (for loanee farmers)
   - Farmer app / CSC / state agriculture office
   - Farmer Corner on https://pmfby.gov.in
3. Provide: farmer name, insurance policy number, crop, area, event date, brief description

Late reporting (after 72 hours) usually leads to claim rejection.

---

## Sum Insured

The sum insured per farmer is based on the **Scale of Finance** for that crop and district (as decided by the State Level Bankers' Committee).

- For **loanee farmers:** sum insured = loan amount (up to the scale of finance)
- For **non-loanee farmers:** sum insured = scale of finance × area sown (usually capped)

Farmers can check their sum insured on the National Crop Insurance Portal after enrolment.

---

## Common Issues and Grievances

Common reasons for claim rejection or non-payment:

- **Late enrolment** — after the cut-off date for that season
- **Late loss reporting** — beyond the 72-hour window for localised losses
- **Wrong crop declared** at enrolment (must match crop actually sown)
- **Land records mismatch** — declared area does not match land documents
- **Bank account issues** — Aadhaar not linked, account frozen

Grievance redressal:
- District Level Grievance Redressal Committee (contact through DAO office)
- State Level Grievance Redressal Committee
- Insurance company customer care
- Online grievance form on https://pmfby.gov.in

## Summary — Farmer's Quick Reference

- **What:** Crop insurance against yield losses (natural calamities, pests, diseases)
- **Farmer pays:** 2% (kharif), 1.5% (rabi), 5% (commercial/horticulture)
- **Who runs it:** Central Government + state-empanelled insurance companies
- **Who's eligible:** Loanee and non-loanee farmers, sharecroppers, tenants
- **Voluntary since 2020** — loanee farmers must opt IN
- **How to enrol:** https://pmfby.gov.in, bank, CSC, or state agriculture office
- **Must remember:** Enrol before season cut-off + report localised losses within 72 hours
- **Status / claims:** pmfby.gov.in Farmer Corner
