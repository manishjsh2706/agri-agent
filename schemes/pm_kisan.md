# PM-Kisan (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

**Also known as:** PM Kisan, प्रधानमंत्री किसान सम्मान निधि, प्रधानमंत्री शेतकरी सन्मान निधी, PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana, PMKSNY.

**Level:** Central Government of India scheme.
**Ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.
**Launched:** 24 February 2019.
**Official website:** https://pmkisan.gov.in
**Official contact page:** https://pmkisan.gov.in/Contacts.aspx
**Last verified:** July 2026.

---

## What is PM-Kisan

PM-Kisan is a Central Government income-support scheme for landholding farmer families in India. Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive direct cash transfers into their bank accounts to help meet farm and household needs.

The scheme is fully funded by the Government of India. There is no cost or premium for farmers to enrol. Payment is made via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly to the farmer's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

---

## Benefit Amount

Eligible farmers receive **Rs 6,000 per year** in **three equal installments of Rs 2,000** each. The three installments are paid every four months:

- **1st installment:** April to July
- **2nd installment:** August to November
- **3rd installment:** December to March

The amount is credited directly to the farmer's bank account via DBT. There is no cash disbursement at any office.

---

## Eligibility

The following farmers are eligible for PM-Kisan:

- All **landholding farmer families** in India (family = husband, wife, and minor children)
- The family must **own cultivable land** (as per state land records)
- The scheme is applicable to farmers **across all states and union territories** of India

**Note on land size limit:** When PM-Kisan was first launched in February 2019, the scheme was restricted to families holding up to 2 hectares of cultivable land. On 1 June 2019, this 2-hectare cap was **removed** — the scheme now covers all landholding farmer families regardless of land size. Some pages on pmkisan.gov.in (including older FAQ entries) still reference the original 2-hectare limit — farmers should refer to the current guidelines. Even farmers owning less than 1 acre are eligible. There is no upper age limit. Both men and women farmers are eligible.

---

## Who is NOT Eligible (Exclusions)

The following categories of persons are **NOT** eligible for PM-Kisan even if they own farmland:

- Institutional landholders (land owned by a company or trust rather than an individual family)
- Farmer families where any member holds a **constitutional post** (e.g., MP, MLA, Minister, Mayor, Zilla Panchayat Chairperson) — current or former
- Serving or retired officers and employees of Central or State Government (**earning Rs 10,000 or more per month** as pension), and PSU / autonomous body / local body employees at Group A / Group B / Group C level
- **Income taxpayers** — anyone who paid income tax in the last assessment year
- Retired pensioners with **pension of Rs 10,000 or more per month**
- Professionals like **doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants, and architects** who are registered with professional bodies and practising

If any single family member falls into an exclusion category, the entire family is ineligible.

## How to Apply

There are three ways to register for PM-Kisan:

**1. Online — Self-registration (easiest)**
- Go to https://pmkisan.gov.in
- Click on **"Farmers Corner"** → **"New Farmer Registration"**
- Enter your Aadhaar number, state, and captcha
- Fill in personal details, bank details, and land ownership details
- Submit the form

**2. Common Service Center (CSC)**
- Visit your nearest CSC / Aaple Sarkar Seva Kendra
- Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE) will help you fill the form
- A small service charge may apply

**3. State Government Agriculture Officer**
- Visit your Taluka or District Agriculture Office
- Officer will register you into the PM-Kisan portal
- Free of cost

After registration, your application is verified by state government officials. Once approved, the first installment is credited to your bank account.

---

## Required Documents

Keep these documents ready before applying:

- **Aadhaar card** (mandatory — must be linked to your bank account)
- **Bank passbook** or bank account details (account number + IFSC code)
- **Land ownership records** (7/12 extract in Maharashtra, Khasra-Khatauni in northern states)
- **Mobile number** (for OTP verification and SMS alerts)
- **Recent passport-size photo**

---

## e-KYC is Mandatory

To keep receiving PM-Kisan installments, farmers **must complete e-KYC** (electronic Know-Your-Customer verification). There are two ways:

- **OTP-based e-KYC:** Available on the PM-Kisan portal — only for farmers whose Aadhaar is linked to their mobile number
- **Biometric e-KYC:** Visit the nearest CSC with your Aadhaar; fingerprint verification is done there

Farmers who do not complete e-KYC will **not** receive future installments. e-KYC only needs to be done once, unless there is a change in Aadhaar details.

---

## How to Check Application or Payment Status

Farmers can check the status of their PM-Kisan application, installments, and e-KYC through:

- **Website:** https://pmkisan.gov.in → "Beneficiary Status" (enter Aadhaar number or bank account)
- **PM-Kisan mobile app:** Download from Google Play Store
- **Official contact page:** https://pmkisan.gov.in/Contacts.aspx — for current helpline numbers and email addresses

The status page shows: registration status, installment credit dates, transaction reference numbers, and any pending KYC or bank issues.

---

## Common Reasons for Non-Payment

If a registered farmer does not receive an installment, common reasons include:

- **e-KYC not completed** — most common reason after 2022
- **Aadhaar not linked to bank account** — DBT fails
- **Wrong bank IFSC or account number** in the application
- **Land ownership not verified** by state government officials
- **Bank account frozen** or inactive
- **Duplicate registration** in the same family

Farmers should first check status on pmkisan.gov.in — the status page usually shows the exact reason for non-payment. Then correct the issue via the portal or a CSC.

## Summary — Farmer's Quick Reference

- **What:** Rs 6,000 per year, direct to bank account
- **Frequency:** 3 installments of Rs 2,000 each, every 4 months
- **Who runs it:** Central Government of India
- **Who's eligible:** All landholding farmer families (with income and job exclusions)
- **How to apply:** https://pmkisan.gov.in, CSC, or state agriculture office
- **Must do:** Complete Aadhaar-based e-KYC, link Aadhaar to bank account
- **Status check:** pmkisan.gov.in "Beneficiary Status" page
