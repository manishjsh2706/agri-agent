# MSP (Minimum Support Price)

**Also known as:** MSP, Minimum Support Price, न्यूनतम समर्थन मूल्य, किमान आधारभूत किंमत, government procurement price.

**Level:** Central Government of India policy (implemented with State Government participation).
**Announced by:** Government of India, based on recommendations from **Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP)**.
**Frequency:** Announced twice a year — before Kharif and Rabi seasons.
**Official source:** https://cacp.dacnet.nic.in (CACP portal) and Ministry of Agriculture releases.
**Last verified:** July 2026.

---

## What is MSP

Minimum Support Price (MSP) is the price at which the Government of India commits to buy specified agricultural commodities from farmers if market prices fall below that level. It is a **safety net** that protects farmers from distress sales when market prices crash.

MSP is NOT a scheme in the classic sense — no application, no registration. It is a **price-policy assurance**. Any farmer growing a covered crop can sell to designated procurement agencies at MSP.

---

## Which Crops Have MSP

MSP is announced for **22 major crops**, divided into two seasons:

**Kharif (monsoon) crops — MSP announced before June:**
- Paddy (common and Grade A)
- Jowar (hybrid and maldandi)
- Bajra
- Ragi
- Maize
- Tur (arhar)
- Moong
- Urad
- Groundnut
- Sunflower seed
- Soybean (yellow)
- Sesamum
- Nigerseed
- Cotton (medium and long staple)

**Rabi (winter) crops — MSP announced before October:**
- Wheat
- Barley
- Gram (chana)
- Masur (lentil)
- Rapeseed and Mustard
- Safflower

**Commercial crops:**
- Sugarcane (Fair and Remunerative Price — FRP — set separately by CACP)
- Copra (dried coconut)
- Jute (retted / raw)
- Cotton (also in kharif list)

---

## How MSP is Determined

MSP calculation follows a formula based on cost of production:

- **A2:** Actual paid-out costs (seed, fertilizer, labour, irrigation, etc.)
- **A2+FL:** A2 + imputed value of family labour
- **C2:** Comprehensive cost — A2+FL + imputed rent on owned land + imputed interest on owned capital

The Government commits to setting MSP at **at least 1.5 times the A2+FL cost** (a 50% margin above paid-out costs and family labour). This 1.5x formula was implemented from 2018-19 onwards.

CACP recommends MSP for each crop; the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) approves and Government of India announces.

---

## How Procurement Works

MSP is enforced through government procurement agencies:

**Wheat and Paddy:**
- **Food Corporation of India (FCI)** — nodal agency
- State agencies (Punjab Grain Procurement Corp, Haryana Warehousing Corp, etc.)
- Procurement at state-designated **mandis and procurement centers**
- Payment directly to farmer's bank account within 48-72 hours

**Pulses, Oilseeds, and Cotton:**
- **NAFED** (National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation)
- **CCI** (Cotton Corporation of India) — for cotton
- Procurement under **PSS (Price Support Scheme)** when market prices fall below MSP
- State-level implementation through NAFED-empanelled centers

**Sugarcane:**
- Direct payment by sugar mills at the FRP (Fair and Remunerative Price)
- FRP is legally binding on sugar mills — payment within 14 days of delivery

---

## How Farmers Sell at MSP

Farmers can sell their crop at MSP through these channels:

**1. State-notified procurement centers (mandis)**
- FCI / state agency procurement centers open during the harvest season
- Farmers bring produce, quality is checked (moisture, foreign matter, grain size)
- If quality meets FAQ (Fair Average Quality), procurement happens at MSP
- Payment to Aadhaar-linked bank account

**2. Under Price Support Scheme (PSS)**
- Activated when market price drops below MSP
- Registered farmers can sell to NAFED / state agency
- Requires prior registration and land documents

**3. Direct sale to sugar mills**
- Only for sugarcane
- Farmer registers with a specific mill (chosen at start of season)
- Mill is legally required to pay FRP within 14 days

---

## Documents Needed for MSP Procurement

Farmers must have these documents ready at the procurement center:

- **Aadhaar card**
- **Land ownership records** (7/12 extract, patta, etc.)
- **Bank passbook** with account number and IFSC (Aadhaar-linked)
- **Registration slip** — many states require prior online registration for the season
- **Crop details** — variety, area sown, expected quantity

Some states have online portals (e.g., Meri Fasal Mera Byora in Haryana, e-Uparjan in MP) where farmers pre-register before the procurement season starts.

## What "at least 1.5x A2+FL" Really Means

The 1.5x formula (50% margin) has been the subject of debate. Key facts:

- **A2+FL** is used as the base — this includes farmer's family labour but NOT imputed land rent or capital interest
- Some farmer groups demand MSP at 1.5 × **C2** (comprehensive cost), which would be a higher price
- Currently the Government uses **A2+FL** as the base for the 1.5x calculation

The actual MSP for each crop is published every year on the CACP website and via Press Information Bureau (PIB) releases from the Government of India.

## Common Farmer Questions

- **"Is MSP legally guaranteed?"** — MSP is a policy assurance, not a legal right (except sugarcane FRP which is legally binding on mills). Government procures at MSP but does not force private traders to buy at MSP.
- **"Can I sell to private buyers if market price is above MSP?"** — Yes. Farmers can sell freely at market rates. MSP kicks in only when market falls below MSP.
- **"Where are procurement centers?"** — Announced by state agriculture departments each season. Check with your Taluka Agriculture Officer or state agriculture portal.
- **"When is payment received?"** — Typically 48-72 hours after procurement, directly into Aadhaar-linked bank account.
- **"Can tenant farmers sell at MSP?"** — Yes, provided they can show valid land use documentation.

## Summary — Farmer's Quick Reference

- **What:** Government-guaranteed minimum price for 22 major crops
- **Who runs it:** Central Government (CACP recommends, GOI announces), state agencies procure
- **Which crops:** 14 kharif + 6 rabi + 2 commercial = 22 crops
- **When announced:** Kharif MSP before June, Rabi MSP before October
- **Formula:** At least 1.5x A2+FL cost of production
- **Who procures:** FCI (wheat/rice), NAFED (pulses/oilseeds), CCI (cotton), sugar mills (sugarcane)
- **How to sell:** State-notified procurement centers with Aadhaar + land records + registration
- **Payment:** Direct to Aadhaar-linked bank account within 48-72 hours (14 days for sugarcane)
