# KCC (Kisan Credit Card)

**Also known as:** KCC, Kisan Credit Card scheme, किसान क्रेडिट कार्ड, शेतकरी क्रेडिट कार्ड, KCC Card.

**Level:** Central Government of India scheme (executed through banks and cooperative societies).
**Ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, in coordination with Reserve Bank of India and NABARD.
**Launched:** 1998 (revamped with interest subvention in 2004; extended to fisheries and animal husbandry in 2019).
**Official information:** https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/Kcc.aspx (KCC section under PM-Kisan portal)
**Last verified:** July 2026.

---

## What is KCC

Kisan Credit Card is a short-term credit product designed for farmers to meet the cultivation and household expenses of a cropping season without going to informal moneylenders. It is provided by public sector banks, private sector banks, regional rural banks (RRBs), and cooperative societies.

A KCC gives the farmer a pre-approved credit limit that can be drawn upon flexibly during the season and repaid after harvest. It is one of the cheapest sources of institutional credit available to Indian farmers.

---

## Interest Rate on KCC Loans

The effective interest rate on KCC loans is one of the cheapest available:

- **Base rate:** Banks charge around **7% per annum** on KCC loans up to Rs 3 lakh
- **Interest subvention:** Central Government provides **2% subvention** on prompt-paying farmers, reducing effective rate to 5%
- **Additional incentive:** For farmers who **repay on or before the due date**, an additional **3% subvention** is provided, bringing the effective interest rate to just **4% per annum**

**Important:** The 3% prompt repayment incentive is available only up to a loan of Rs 3 lakh and only for the first Rs 3 lakh of larger loans.

---

## Purposes for Which KCC Can Be Used

KCC funds can be used for:

- **Short-term crop production loans** — seeds, fertilizer, pesticides, labour, irrigation
- **Post-harvest expenses** — including marketing
- **Household consumption needs**
- **Working capital** for farm-related activities
- **Investment credit** for allied activities like dairy, poultry, fisheries (limited)
- **Consumption loan** component (up to a small % of overall limit)

Since 2019, KCC has been extended to cover:
- **Fisheries** (marine and inland fishermen, fish farmers)
- **Animal husbandry** (dairy farmers, poultry farmers, livestock rearers)

---

## Who is Eligible for KCC

The following categories can apply for a KCC:

- All **individual farmer owner-cultivators** growing any crop
- **Joint borrowers** and **tenant farmers** (with valid tenancy or oral lease documentation)
- **Sharecroppers and lessee farmers**
- **Self-Help Groups (SHGs)** and Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) of farmers
- **Fish farmers, fishermen, and inland fisheries operators** (since 2019)
- **Dairy farmers, poultry farmers, and livestock rearers** (since 2019)

There is no minimum landholding size required. Small and marginal farmers are actively targeted.

---

## Credit Limit and Amount

The credit limit on a KCC is decided based on:

- **Scale of Finance** for the crop and district (fixed by the State Level Bankers' Committee)
- **Area of cultivation** owned or leased by the farmer
- **Cropping pattern** (single crop, multiple crops)
- **Post-harvest and consumption needs** — added as a percentage
- **Insurance premium (PMFBY)** — automatically included

For example: If Scale of Finance for wheat is Rs 40,000 per hectare and the farmer cultivates 2 hectares, base crop credit = Rs 80,000. Additional 10-25% is added for post-harvest, consumption, and insurance, bringing total limit to around Rs 1 lakh.

Credit limit is typically **increased by 10% every year for 5 years** (built-in growth).

## How to Apply for KCC

There are three ways to apply:

**1. Bank branch — direct application**
- Visit any bank offering KCC (public sector, private, RRB, cooperative)
- Ask for the Kisan Credit Card application form
- Submit filled form with required documents
- Bank verifies land records with revenue authorities
- KCC is issued within 15 days (as per RBI guidelines)

**2. Online through PM-Kisan portal**
- PM-Kisan beneficiaries can apply for KCC through the Farmer Corner
- Go to https://www.pmkisan.gov.in/Kcc.aspx
- Download simplified 1-page application form
- Fill and submit at nearest bank branch

**3. Common Service Center (CSC) or Agriculture Officer**
- CSC operators can help fill and forward applications
- State agriculture officers can facilitate applications

Under a special drive tied to PM-Kisan, banks are asked to sanction KCC to eligible farmers within **14 days** of receiving a complete application.

---

## Required Documents

Standard documents needed for KCC application:

- **Filled KCC application form**
- **Aadhaar card**
- **PAN card** (mandatory for loans above Rs 5 lakh)
- **Land ownership records** (7/12 extract in Maharashtra, patta / khasra in other states)
- **Passport-size photographs**
- **Bank account details** at the same or any bank
- For **tenant / lessee farmers:** valid tenancy agreement or affidavit of oral lease
- For **fisheries / animal husbandry:** ownership proof of activity (fish pond, dairy shed, etc.)

For loans up to Rs 1.6 lakh, banks cannot ask for collateral (RBI directive) — the loan is based on land + crop as security.

---

## Repayment Terms

KCC has flexible repayment aligned with cropping cycles:

- **For crop loans:** repayable after harvest of the crop (kharif season loans typically by March, rabi season loans by September)
- **Grace period:** No additional grace period; interest accumulates after due date
- **Interest subvention:** Only applies if repaid ON OR BEFORE the due date. Late repayment loses the 3% incentive
- **Renewal:** KCC is typically valid for 5 years and renewable. Credit limit is enhanced annually

If a farmer defaults, the KCC is downgraded to NPA (Non-Performing Asset) and full commercial interest rate (typically 12-14%) is charged. Recovery follows normal banking process.

---

## Collateral-Free Limit

Under RBI's simplified scheme:

- KCC loans **up to Rs 1.6 lakh** are **collateral-free** (no land mortgage needed)
- Loans between Rs 1.6 lakh and Rs 3 lakh may require hypothecation of crops
- Loans above Rs 3 lakh may require additional collateral (usually the land itself)

For small and marginal farmers, this collateral-free limit is a major benefit — no risk of losing land if the crop fails.

---

## Common Issues

Common reasons for KCC rejection or delays:

- **Land records not updated** — village revenue authority needs to verify current ownership
- **Existing loan overdue** at another bank or cooperative society
- **Poor CIBIL score** — even for farmers, banks now check credit history
- **Incomplete tenancy documentation** for tenant / sharecropper farmers
- **Aadhaar not linked to bank account**

Grievance redressal:
- Bank's Grievance Officer (branch level)
- Bank's Regional / Zonal Office
- Banking Ombudsman (RBI)
- District Level Consultative Committee (DLCC) meetings

---

## Summary — Farmer's Quick Reference

- **What:** Short-term credit card for farmers — crop loans, working capital, consumption
- **Interest rate:** Effectively 4% per annum on prompt repayment (up to Rs 3 lakh)
- **Who runs it:** Public / private / cooperative banks, executed under RBI + NABARD guidelines
- **Who's eligible:** All farmers, tenants, sharecroppers, SHGs, fisheries, dairy, poultry
- **Credit limit:** Based on cropping pattern and Scale of Finance
- **Collateral-free:** Up to Rs 1.6 lakh
- **How to apply:** Bank branch, PM-Kisan portal, or CSC
- **Sanctioning time:** 14 days (RBI directive)

---

*Note: KCC interest rates, subvention rates, and RBI directives change from time to time. Farmers should confirm current interest rate, subvention, and eligibility with their bank before applying. State-specific details (Scale of Finance) also vary.*
